# rediSONG Privacy Policy Effective date: 6th October, 2026 rediSONG is a songbook and performance app for organising songs, set lists, lyrics, chords, and related song information. ## Information stored on your device rediSONG stores the songbook information you create or import locally on your device. This may include song titles, lyrics, chords, set lists, tags, notes, YouTube links, display preferences, and foot-pedal settings. This information is not sent to rediSONG or to a developer-operated server. rediSONG does not currently provide user accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, or tracking. ## Imported and exported files If you choose to import a Desktop snapshot, rediSONG reads the file you select and stores its contents locally on your device. Exported or imported songbook files remain under your control. ## YouTube If you choose to open a YouTube link from within a song, rediSONG loads YouTube video content using YouTube’s embedded player or opens the selected link externally. YouTube may collect information in accordance with its own privacy policy when you use its services. rediSONG does not control YouTube’s data practices. ## Permissions rediSONG uses Internet access only to load user-requested YouTube content and open external links. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or device storage beyond the files you explicitly choose to import or export. ## Data sharing rediSONG does not sell, rent, or share your songbook data with third parties. Any interaction with YouTube occurs only after you choose to open a YouTube link. ## Changes to this policy If rediSONG adds accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, or other data-sharing features in the future, this privacy policy will be updated before those features are released. ## Contact For privacy questions, contact: Philip Bruce redisongapp@gmail.com
